Wanderlust

The problem
Travellers need one place to search for stays, save favourites and read reviews.
What I built
A full-stack stay-listing app. Users sign up and log in with JWT cookie sessions, search by destination, filter by category (Trending, Castles, Camping and more), and save listings to a wishlist.
